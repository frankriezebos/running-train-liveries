How to install a livery texture?

Very easy! Just download the texture and place it in the appropriate folder for your train model within C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\RUNNING TRAIN\RunningTrain\Content\UGC\Textures. Replace it with an empty tex.jpg file.
